TRANSPARENCY BY DESIGN

How the score works

The current prototype uses a transparent skill-coverage calculation: matched required skills divided by the total required skills, multiplied by 100. It is an explainable baseline, not a trained model or hiring prediction. The system displays matched and missing skills so users can inspect the result.

Future extensions may include semantic NLP matching, verified live vacancy feeds, richer resume parsing and recruiter-side candidate review.
